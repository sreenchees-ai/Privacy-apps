Privacy policy

ScreenLink lets one phone show its screen live to another phone or a computer. This page explains exactly what that involves.

Effective 5 October 2026. Applies to the ScreenLink Android app and its web viewer.

What ScreenLink uses

  • Your live screen, only while you share and after you approve Android's screen-capture prompt
  • Your device's pairing code, to connect the two devices
  • Network connection details (such as IP addresses) needed to deliver the video
  • Advertising data handled by Google AdMob, as described below

Never collected

  • No account, name, email or phone number
  • No screen recordings, screenshots, photos or videos
  • No contacts, files, camera, microphone or location
  • No sale of your data, and no tracking for our own purposes

Who we are

ScreenLink is developed and published by Abrar Ali (rebrar.in), based in Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir, India. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the ScreenLink developer. “You” means anyone who uses the ScreenLink app or its web viewer.

ScreenLink does not have user accounts. You never sign up, log in or give us your name, email address or phone number.

Your screen content

ScreenLink only captures your screen after you tap START SHARING and approve Android's own screen-capture prompt. While sharing is on, Android shows a notification and a status-bar indicator, and you can stop at any time with STOP SHARING, the notification's Stop button or Android's own controls.

  • Your screen is sent as live video directly to the one device that connected with your pairing code.
  • The video is encrypted in transit using the standard WebRTC protocols (DTLS-SRTP).
  • Nothing is recorded or saved, on your phone, on the viewer's device or on our servers. To show a still screen to someone who joins, the app keeps only the most recent frame in your phone's memory, and discards it when sharing stops.
  • Only one viewer can be connected at a time, and your phone shows when someone is watching.

Anything visible on your screen while sharing, including notifications and messages, can be seen by the viewer. Only share your code with people you trust, and stop sharing when you're done.

Pairing codes

Each device gets a random 8-character pairing code the first time it's needed. The code is created on your device, is not linked to your identity, and is stored only in the app's private storage. It is excluded from Android backups and device-to-device transfers.

When you share or connect, the code is sent to our signalling server so the two devices can find each other. Anyone who knows your code can ask to watch while you are sharing, so treat it like a temporary key.

Signalling server

Before two devices can connect, they exchange a few technical messages through our signalling server. These include the pairing code, a random session token, and WebRTC connection details, which contain network addresses such as IP addresses.

  • All of this is held in the server's memory only, never written to a database or file.
  • A session is deleted as soon as sharing stops, 30 seconds after the sharing phone loses its connection, or after 8 hours at the most.
  • To prevent people guessing codes, the server counts failed connection attempts per IP address for up to one minute.
  • Pairing codes are never written to server logs. Our hosting provider may keep standard network logs, such as IP addresses and connection times, for security and reliability, under its own policies.
  • Your screen's video does not pass through the signalling server.

STUN and TURN servers

To find a network route between the two devices, the app contacts STUN servers (by default, Google's public STUN servers). These servers see your device's public IP address, as any internet service does.

If a direct connection isn't possible, for example on some mobile networks, the encrypted video is passed through a TURN relay server operated by us or by a hosting provider we use. The relay forwards encrypted data and cannot see your screen. It uses short-lived credentials and keeps nothing after the session, apart from any standard network logs kept by the hosting provider.

Watching on a computer

The viewer can also watch in a web browser on a computer. The web viewer works the same way as the app: it connects with the pairing code, receives live encrypted video, and saves nothing. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and loads nothing from third-party websites.

Advertising

ScreenLink is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Ads appear on the home screen and the code entry screen, and occasionally after a viewing session has ended.

Ads are never shown while your phone is sharing its screen, and never over a live remote screen.

What Google collects

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, general device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google processes this data as described in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the app asks for your consent or choices using Google's consent tool before ads are personalized.
  • Where required, a Privacy & ad settings link on the home screen lets you change your choices at any time.
  • On any Android device, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact wording varies by device).

We do not receive your advertising ID or personal data from Google. We only see combined reports, such as how many ads were shown.

Security

Live video is encrypted between devices with DTLS-SRTP, the security built into WebRTC. Release versions of the app connect to the signalling server only over an encrypted connection (WSS). Pairing codes are random and the server limits repeated wrong guesses.

No system is perfectly secure. Keep your pairing code private and stop sharing when you're finished.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop sharing at any time; the session and its server data are deleted.
  • Delete your pairing code by clearing ScreenLink's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Change ad choices through the Privacy & ad settings link (where shown) or your device's ads settings.

Because we do not create accounts or keep session data after a session ends, we usually hold no information that can be linked to you. Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU and UK GDPR, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

ScreenLink is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used ScreenLink in a way that concerns you, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how ScreenLink handles data, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
